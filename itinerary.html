Important Notes – Buses, Check-in & Timing

G8 Cabin Sleeper Bus (Hanoi ↔ Sapa) — exact booking details
• Outward (13 Nov 23:15): Pickup from Prince II Hotel, 42B P. Hàng Giầy, Phố cổ Hà Nội. Drop-off at Freesia Hotel, 3 Vuon Treo, Sapa.
• Be ready at the hotel lobby by ~22:30–22:45 (confirm exact shuttle time with G8 the day before).
• Return (15 Nov 13:00): Pickup from Freesia Hotel, 3 Vuon Treo. Drop-off at Prince II Hotel, Hanoi Old Quarter.
• Be at Freesia by ~12:30 for the 13:00 return.
• Lower single cabins ×4. Luggage ≤20 kg + small bag. No onboard toilet — rest stops only.
• Hotline (problems): +84 382 995 611 / +84 358 482 611 (WhatsApp/Viber). Contact promptly if any issue.
