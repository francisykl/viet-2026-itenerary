Comfort-first routes

These routes use one natural walking loop at a time, then a planned rest. Times are intentionally padded for four people and six bags.

12 Nov
Arrival buffer

HAN → Old Quarter hotel

  • 20:30 landing → allow 45–75 min for immigration and bags.
  • Book a 7-seater transfer for roughly 21:30–22:00; allow 35–60 min to the Old Quarter depending on traffic.
  • Food pick: simple nearby banh mi, pho, or street food; keep it light after the flight.
  • Eat within a 5–10 minute walk of the hotel; no sightseeing commitment tonight.
13 Nov
Walk loop · 4–5 hrs

Egg coffee → cathedral → shirts → banh mi

  • 08:30 breakfast near the hotel.
  • 09:00–10:00 Cafe Giang; 10:10–10:35 St Joseph’s Cathedral.
  • 10:45–12:15 walk Hang Gai first for nicer ready-made shirts, then Hang Dao/Hang Ngang for casual options.
  • 12:15–13:00 food pick: Banh Mi 25 or Banh My Mama; return to the hotel by 13:30.
  • Keep 13:30–19:30 for shower, luggage, dinner, and a calm bus check-in. Be at the bus point 30 minutes early; take a light dinner before boarding.
14 Nov
Sapa · rest protected

Room → Fansipan → town → hotpot

  • Arrive around 05:00–06:00; go directly to the room booked from 13 Nov. Sleep until at least 09:00.
  • 09:00–09:30 breakfast at Bamboo Sapa or a simple noodle/rice dish near Sun Plaza.
  • 09:30–14:00 Fansipan cable car and summit. Allow time for the transfer, queues, cable car, summit steps, photos, and weather delays.
  • If visibility is poor, switch to Cat Cat Village instead.
  • 14:00–16:00 food pick and hotel rest, then 16:00–17:30 Sun Plaza → Sapa Square → Love Market area.
  • 18:00 onward Sapa Night Market and Black Chicken Hotpot.
15 Nov
Transfer buffer

Sapa slow morning → Hanoi

  • 09:00–09:45 coffee and views at Viettrekking Coffee; 09:45–10:30 walk Sun Plaza → Sapa Square → central ethnic market.
  • Food pick: early lunch near Sun Plaza, then return to Bamboo Sapa for bags and checkout.
  • 10:30–11:30 pack and check out; keep bags with the hotel if needed.
  • Reach the bus pickup point by 12:30 for a 13:00–14:00 departure.
  • Allow 6–8 hours including traffic and stops. On arrival, check in, eat nearby, and do not schedule another attraction.
16–17 Nov
Cruise buffer

Hanoi hotel → Halong Bay → Hanoi

  • 16 Nov: be ready in the hotel lobby 30 minutes before the cruise pickup window; keep passports and one small day bag accessible.
  • Food pick: use the cruise’s included breakfast, lunch, dinner, and cooking activity; budget only for drinks unless the package says otherwise.
  • 17 Nov: after returning around 16:00–17:00, take a full rest evening. Keep large luggage at the Hanoi hotel if the cruise operator allows it.
18 Nov
Main city route

Old Quarter shirts → local lunch → relaxed evening

  • 09:00 breakfast and coffee near the hotel.
  • 10:00–12:00 repeat the best shirt streets: Hang Gai for quality, Hang Dao/Hang Ngang for casual choices.
  • 12:15–13:30 food pick: choose Bun Cha Huong Lien or Pho Thin; allow 30–45 minutes each way if using Grab.
  • 14:00–16:00 Hang Ma souvenirs, Maison Marou, or hotel rest.
  • 18:30 water puppet show or Train Street only if desired; otherwise choose Cha Ca Thang Long for dinner and keep the evening easy.
19 Nov
Airport buffer

Final shopping → HAN

  • 09:30–10:30 final quality-shirt pass on Hang Gai; 10:30–11:30 casual shirts and souvenirs on Hang Dao/Hang Ngang.
  • Food pick: 11:45 pho at Pho Thin or a quick banh mi near the hotel.
  • 12:30 return for bags and check-out.
  • 13:00–16:30 free buffer for shower, packing, traffic, or a short café break.
  • Leave around 17:00–17:30 for the 21:00 international flight; target arrival at the airport by roughly 18:30.
