14 Nov · Full day Sapa

Fansipan main
Fansipan Cable Car
Fansipan Summit
Sapa Stone Church
Sapa Night Market
06:45–07:30 — Hotel breakfast / Sun Plaza area. Early start.
07:30–12:30 — ★ FANSIPAN (main plan). Cable car + summit. Weather check with hotel first if foggy. Three tickets: mountain train + cable car + summit steps/funicular ≈ RM 150–230. Best clear views in morning.
If fog Do Mong Village / coaster first, or just town loop. Don’t force the mountain.
13:30–15:30 — Mong Village block: Alpine Coaster (Ban Mong, ~RM 33, 3–5 min ride) + walk village lanes (Black Hmong houses, free) + herbal tea (RM 3–7). One road, no backtracking.
Optional Moana Sapa photo park (golden hand, infinity swing, RM 13–17) or rose garden if on the way.
16:30–17:30 — Stone Church + Sun Plaza square. Free photos.
17:30–20:00 — Sapa Night Market + dinner.
Food Red Dzao House (black chicken hotpot), A Phủ (salmon hotpot), or market snacks (cơm lam, xôi bảy màu, grilled pig). No Michelin in Sapa.
Optional only if ahead of schedule: Muong Hoa valley viewpoints or Cat Cat Village (opposite direction). Don’t try both after Fansipan.
